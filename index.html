Based on the statutory Key Information Memorandum (KIM) and Scheme Information Document (SID), the exit load provisions are structured as follows:

check_circle 1.00% Exit Load: Applies if redeemed or switched out on or before completion of 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment.

info Units allotted via Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) or Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) are evaluated individually on a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) basis.
